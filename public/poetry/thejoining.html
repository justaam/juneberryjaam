the joining

posted 3 november 2026 - tuesday @723PM


with the day, there comes the night
with the dark, there may still be night
this light, gathered forth, into a shared hearth
all started from one little spark


it is time once again to open the mounds
to bring forward what came before
to beckon the hallowed spirits
to embrance the sounds
the sounds and senses across the veil
the portal to the otherworld
appease them, we must
their memory, their lives, we dutifully hail


a seat at the table
there sits a memory
of those long gone, of those that we dearly miss
of those that we hold dear, those we may have kissed
kissed by life's end, yet these souls will return
return now to their home, time together earned


the darker half approaches, the end of summer here
it is time to ponder with this veil now sheer
conceal your being, beware what walks
for now you may hear the otherworldly talk


we, as little lights, must gather by the fire
and do what we can to gain none of the ire
from harmful disease, from bountiless harvests
honor what has been
honor what will be
honor the ancestors, embrace the darkness
for together, by this bonfire,
we shall find good strength and catharsis


we must let the light of our souls be exposed
to be gathered, to be collected
for we all have our own roles
our roles that we play in this short, fickle life
together, our joined light becomes a warm shared flame
a fire that not one, but us all, shall tame


the sting of loss, the consuming feeling of fear
it comes for us all
we should know that our time is limited here
have we not felt the pain of grief? (let go)
have we not shed enough tears? (let go)


do you feel it?
the thinness of reality in the air
our breaths heaved as one
our existences we share
share, this space, we do with those from beyond
the veil is open! our chance is here!
for stories of the past, for long awaited meetings
for time to spend with those we hold most dear


there is calm to be found in the darkness
there is peace to be found in the silence
there is serenity in honoring memories
there is acceptance in open vulnerability


embrance the long night
and let your light shine ever so bright.
