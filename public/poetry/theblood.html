the blood in between my legs

posted 21 february 2026 - saturday @930PM


the gift of life, they said, is the ability to give birth life as a woman
I was 9... I matured early
my eyes haunted and glazed over at the stain on my Winnie the Pooh blankets
I ached so much, I begged and pleaded for it to be over
for seven days I cried a river and bled a river
I would sit in the shower and refuse to leave,
fearful I would stain yet another set of clothes or bed sheets


once it stopped, I was told it would happen again and again, month after month
I dreaded it and began to hate the color reminder
others at school spoke of it in hushed tones
elders in my life would say that I am all grown up now
that this is how my body was preparing itself for an eventual pregnancy
when I was 14, a classmate of mine got pregnant
for something supposedly so cherished, why did everyone around us call her a whore?
her family moved away


this gift could only be celebrated under certain terms, it seems
everyone wouldonly speak positively of the possibility of it
yet shame the girls who did it too young
getting your cycle apparently meant you were ripe for the taking
no one shamed the boys for their misconduct, their actions brushed off
the spotlight of shame only on the girls who couldn't say no


when I screamed, no one came


only the girls went into the after school rooms for counseling
you could hear the weeping down the hall
each time I went, I would just sit and enjoy the peace
the peace of being left alone, away from the gaze and graze
ignoring unwanted attention, noticing how tight looks were exchanged between teachers
red ran down my legs for two reasons


my future was assumed to one day be at a man's side
to provide him many children and a fruitful legacy
boys would brag to girls about how they would provide all for them
all I could think about was how their hands felt on my hips
I could not imagine being a wife then
I was still a child
but here, they force children to play in an adult world
I wasn't a child to them the moment I started bleeding


my mother cried over and over when I finally told her what happened
the blood in between my legs caused by puberty, and then later caused by a man's sick, perverse greed
I remember begging to God, "I don't want to be a whore"
I refused and refuse to be defined by a man's sins
my prayers were answered, and I was saved from eternal damnation
the bruises fade, but the ache persists.
