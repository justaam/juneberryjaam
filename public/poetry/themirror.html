the mirror

posted 27 october 2026 - tuesday @730PM


waking up is unpleasant
there is a stillness to my bones
an ache to my muscles
present reminders to my existence
i didn't ask to live
but dying is up to me


stumbling around my home
i make way for the bathroom
my steps slow
anticipation in my throat
for inside there is a stranger


i open the door and there she is
standing, staring, stalking
she is the woman that i left behind


i can't meet her gaze
though i know that she is there
perhaps she feels the same pains i do
or maybe she cries the same tears
or has the same frown lines
or feels trapped, the way that i do
her, trapped in glass
me, trapped in a body


avoiding her is not easy
she follows me everywhere
i just don't want to look into her sad, sad eyes


getting ready is a challenge
the day does not wait
and time is a cruel master
she and i have aged together
but will we pass on the same?


will her cheeks become hollow?
will her skin become gaunt?
will the pain finally fade away?
will she finally be free?
free from sorrow,
free from guilt,
free from expectation,
free from me?


i look up


the man that i am
and the woman who is
both existing, both lost
both wondering about a world that is different
a world where they may both exist


the greatest ache lies not in my bones
or my muscles, or my feet, or my hands
but in my heart, soul, and mind


a reflection is all she is
a hiding man is all i am
